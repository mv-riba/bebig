Delete your Bebig account

Bebig, by Maria [SURNAME] · Last updated 23 November 2026

You can delete your Bebig account and its data at any time. You don't need the app installed: Bebig also runs in any web browser.

Option 1: delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open bebig.app in any browser, or open the Bebig app.
  2. Sign in with the email address and password for the account you want to delete. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.
  3. Optional: to keep a copy of your records, go to Settings → Data export & deletion and tap Export JSON or Export CSV.
  4. Go to Settings → Account and tap Delete account.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you can't sign in, email privacy@bebig.app with the subject "Delete my Bebig account". Send it from the email address you use for Bebig, so we know the request is really from you. We'll delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address, password and settings.
  • Every baby profile you own, with all its records (feeds, sleep, nappies, growth, extra trackers, notes and photo), for everyone who has access to it. If someone else should keep these records, export them first, or ask them to export theirs.
  • Your memberships of other people's baby profiles, and all invitations sent to your email address.
  • Your Bebig Plus trial or early-access status, AI consent and assistant usage counts.
  • The copy of your data on that device.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Entries you logged on someone else's baby profile stay with that baby, because they're part of the baby's record, and the family still needs them. Your name is removed from them and they show as "Former caregiver". Notes you wrote in them stay. To have them removed, ask the profile owner or email us.
  • Sharing log: records of invitations and access changes are kept for up to 12 months for security, with your name and email address removed.
  • Backups and provider logs: if our database host keeps backups, your data drops out of them within 30 days. Technical logs at our providers expire on their own schedule, typically within a few weeks.
  • Purchase records: once paid subscriptions start, records needed for accounting are kept as long as the law requires (in Finland, usually up to 6 years).

Subscriptions: deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions first. (Paid subscriptions aren't available yet.)

Delete some data, but keep your account

  • A single entry: open it and tap the bin icon.
  • All of one baby's data (owners only): Settings → Data export & deletion → Delete all baby data. This deletes the profile and every record for all members.
  • Stop sharing: caregivers can leave a profile, and owners can revoke access, in Settings → Shared access.
  • AI phrasing: turn it off in Settings → Privacy.
