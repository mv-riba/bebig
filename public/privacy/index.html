  • Bebig stores what you log about your baby so you, and the people you invite, can see it on any device. Some of it is health-related, so we treat all baby records as sensitive.

  • You can export everything and delete your account at any time, in the app or at bebig.app/delete-account.
  • Bebig is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.

1. Who we are

Bebig (the app at bebig.app and the Bebig app on Google Play) is made and run by Marija Riba, an independent developer based in Finland who trades as MVR Studios (Finnish Business ID 3485332-7). She is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In this policy, "Bebig", "we" and "us" mean her.

Questions about privacy: privacy@bebig.app.

2. What we collect

You give us

  • Account details: your email address, your name, and your password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form).
  • Baby profile: your baby's name, date of birth, sex, gestational age at birth, time zone, birth weight, length and head circumference, feeding preference and, if you add one, a photo.
  • Care records: feeds (type, times, durations, breast side, amounts, observations), nappies (time, colour, size, texture, and wet nappies if you turn them on), sleep (times, pauses), growth measurements, extra trackers you set up (for example a daily vitamin, baths or congestion) and any notes you write.
  • Sharing: the email addresses of people you invite, their role and whether they accepted.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to our support or privacy addresses.

Feeding, nappy, sleep, growth and tracker records can reveal information about your baby's health. We treat them as health data, a special category of personal data under the GDPR. Please only write in notes what you're comfortable storing.

Created when you use Bebig

  • Plan details: whether your family has Bebig Plus, through early access, a trial or (later) a subscription, with start and end dates. When paid subscriptions start, we'll also store the purchase reference Google Play gives us to confirm a purchase. We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • Settings: your app preferences, and whether and when you agreed to AI phrasing (see section 7).
  • Security records: a log of sharing changes (invitations sent, accepted, revoked), a daily count of assistant requests, and the technical logs our providers keep when you connect, such as IP address, device and browser type, and time.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and Bebig doesn't set cookies. We don't collect your location, contacts or anything else from your phone.

4. Who can see your data

  • People you invite. A baby profile is visible only to its owner and to caregivers who have accepted an invitation. They can see and edit the profile and all its records, and see who logged what. Owners can revoke access at any time, and caregivers can leave. This is enforced by the database, not just by the app.
  • Our service providers, listed in the next section, only to run Bebig for us.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We never sell your data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for marketing by others.

6. Where your data is stored

Your account and baby records are stored in the EU (Ireland). Some providers above are US companies, and some data (such as connection details, or text for AI phrasing if you turn it on) may be processed in the United States. When that happens, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with additional safeguards such as encryption in transit.

7. The assistant and AI

The assistant answers questions such as "when was the last feed?" by calculating directly from your records on your device. The numbers always come from your records, never from an AI model.

AI phrasing is optional and off by default. If you have Bebig Plus and turn it on (Settings → Privacy), your question and the answer the app has already calculated are sent to Anthropic's Claude model, only to reword the answer more naturally. Your raw records aren't sent. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models and deletes it after a short retention period under its commercial terms. You can turn it off at any time. AI phrasing isn't switched on in Bebig yet; this section explains how it will work when it is.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and baby data: for as long as your account and the baby profile exist.
  • Deleted entries: when you delete a single entry, it disappears from the app straight away. A deleted copy stays in the database so that other devices can sync the deletion, until the baby profile is deleted.
  • Sharing log: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Assistant request counts: until your account is deleted.
  • Provider technical logs: as set by each provider, typically a few days to a few weeks.
  • Backups: if our database host keeps backups, deleted data drops out of them as they're replaced, normally within 30 days.
  • Purchase records (once subscriptions start): as long as accounting law requires, which in Finland is usually up to 6 years.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to help you, and no more than 2 years after the conversation ends.

9. Deleting your data

  • Delete a baby's data: the profile owner can delete a baby profile and all its records for everyone (Settings → Data export & deletion).
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account, in the app or at bebig.app in any browser. It takes effect immediately. Full steps, including how to ask by email, are at bebig.app/delete-account.

When you delete your account:

  • Your account, name and email address, and every baby profile you own, with all its records, are deleted for everyone.
  • Your pending invitations and memberships are removed.
  • Entries you logged on someone else's baby profile stay with that baby, because they're part of the baby's record and the family still needs them. Your name is removed from them, and they show as "Former caregiver". Any notes you wrote in those entries stay. If you'd like them removed, ask the profile owner to delete them, or contact us.
  • The sharing log keeps the event but no longer says who did it.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data (most of it you can edit yourself in the app);
  • delete your data;
  • take your data with you (Settings → Data export & deletion → Export JSON or CSV);
  • restrict or object to some processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

Email privacy@bebig.app from your account's email address to use any of these rights. We'll reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Finland that's the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman. You can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to every baby profile is checked by the database itself, so one family can't see another's data. Sign-up and password reset are protected by CAPTCHA, and the app only loads code from its own site and the CAPTCHA provider. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Children

Bebig is for parents and caregivers aged 18 or over. Information about babies is entered by their parents or caregivers, who are responsible for it. Babies and children don't use Bebig themselves.

13. Data on your device

To work offline and stay fast, Bebig keeps a copy of your baby records and your sign-in session in your browser's or phone's local storage. Signing out removes it. Optional reminders run on your device only. If you share a device, sign out when you're done.

14. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they apply. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
