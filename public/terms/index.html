2. Who can use Bebig

You must be 18 or over to create an account. Bebig is for parents and caregivers to record information about babies they look after.

3. Your account

  • Give a real email address you can access. It's used to confirm your account and reset your password.
  • Keep your password private, and sign out on shared devices. You're responsible for what happens in your account.
  • Tell us at support@bebig.app if you think someone else has used your account.

4. Not medical advice

Bebig shows totals, charts, WHO growth percentiles, patterns, estimates of likely next feeds or sleeps, and general notices (for example about nappy colours). These are based only on what you record and on general reference data. They can be wrong or incomplete, and they don't take your baby's full health into account.

Bebig doesn't provide medical, nursing or feeding advice, doesn't diagnose or treat anything, and isn't a medical device. Always follow the advice of your healthcare professionals, and don't delay seeking care because of something you did or didn't see in Bebig.

5. Your data

  • What you record stays yours. You give us permission to store, sync and process it only so we can run Bebig for you and the people you share it with, as the privacy policy describes.
  • You can export your records (JSON or CSV) and delete them or your account at any time. See how to delete your account.
  • Only add information you have the right to store, about babies you care for.

6. Sharing with caregivers

  • The person who creates a baby profile is its owner. The owner can invite caregivers, revoke their access and delete the profile and all its records.
  • Caregivers who accept an invitation can see and edit the profile and all its records. Only invite people you trust with this information.
  • A caregiver can leave a profile at any time. If a caregiver deletes their account, the entries they logged stay with the baby as "Former caregiver".
  • If the owner deletes the profile, or their account, the profile and its records are deleted for everyone. Caregivers should export anything they want to keep.

7. Free plan and Bebig Plus

Always free: logging feeds, sleep, nappies (including wet nappies), growth and timers; health notices; WHO growth charts; 7-day trends; history; export; and sharing with one caregiver.

Bebig Plus adds features such as predictions, AI phrasing in the assistant, more caregivers, all of a family's babies, and extra trackers (such as bath, congestion and your own trackers). One plan covers every baby profile the owner's account owns and everyone who cares for them. The current list of Plus features is shown in the app on the Bebig Plus page.

  • Early access: Bebig Plus is free for everyone until 31 December 2026. We'll tell you before early access ends.
  • Free trial: after early access, each family can try Plus free for 14 days, once, after logging for 10 days. No payment details are needed, and it doesn't turn into a paid plan by itself.
  • When Plus ends (after early access, a trial or a subscription), nothing is deleted. Plus features lock again. Extra trackers and their entries stay visible, editable and in your export, but new extra trackers and entries need Plus. Caregivers beyond the free limit are paused, not removed, and get access back automatically if Plus returns.
